# Architecture Lineage

```text
World 6 — Being
What remains stable when cognition/provider changes?

World 7 — Becoming
How can a persistent digital entity develop and reconstruct?

World 8 — Governed Becoming
Who measures, proposes, evaluates, promotes and records accepted change?

World 9 — Accountable Principalhood
Who carries objectives, obligations, bounded authority, resources and accountability while brains, workers and engines act on its behalf?
```

R0.1 treats the World 9 name as the research/project lineage. Whether the final evidence supports a distinct generational boundary or a World 8.x Principal Layer remains an explicit deferred question.
