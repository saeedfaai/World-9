# Public Review Request — World 9 / R0.1

World 9 / R0.1 is published as a **research architecture baseline**, not a production system and not a consciousness/AGI claim.

We invite adversarial review on the following questions:

1. Is the Principal definition operationally distinct from a persistent manager agent?
2. Are layered accountability and remediation semantics sufficiently complete?
3. Can multidimensional delegated authority still be laundered or compositionally escalated?
4. Does `Commitment ≠ Task` close enough real-world obligation failure modes, including apparent external promises?
5. Are the structural/semantic Objective Refinement boundaries falsifiable and safe from LLM backdoors?
6. Does the World 8 ↔ World 9 Binding Contract preserve the five-plane Separation of Duties without creating a hidden sixth plane?
7. Is stop-the-world fenced migration sufficient to establish the limited claim of canonical continuity under exclusive authority transfer?
8. Are shared-engine isolation and failure-dependency semantics strong enough for multiple Principals?
9. Can the epistemic governance model still be captured through evidence selection, correlated evaluators, or UI framing?
10. Which claims appear non-novel in prior literature, and which integrated combination—if any—deserves further novelty testing?
