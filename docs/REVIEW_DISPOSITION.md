# Review Disposition — R0.0 → R0.1

External advisory reviews considered: DeepSeek, Claude, Grok and Perplexity. Their findings were advisory inputs; final architecture decisions are governed by the project.

## Accepted into R0.1

1. Principal needed a stricter distinction from World 8 Entity/Manager Agent.
2. Accountability needed a layered graph, not a vague terminal sink.
3. Governance actors require attributable bindings.
4. Authority subset on a capability bitset was insufficient; multidimensional attenuation is required.
5. Aggregate risk from combinations of grants/actions requires explicit policy.
6. Objective refinement must separate deterministic structural checks from evidence-dependent semantic assessment.
7. Protected metrics require explicit definitions, methodology, sources and evaluation horizons.
8. Commitment lifecycle needs acceptance/binding basis and richer states.
9. Apparent external promises by Workers require communication/provenance policy.
10. Budget model needs reservation settlement, binding exposure and future-period exposure.
11. Budget shrink requires explicit overhang semantics.
12. Constraint hierarchy must become a graph with same-class conflict handling.
13. External `LAW` assertions require verified provenance.
14. Jurisdiction conflicts require applicability/escalation policy rather than universal ranking.
15. Succession requires office/holder separation, dispute state and no auto-coronation.
16. Continuity claim is limited to canonical continuity, not metaphysical sameness.
17. Migration requires durable fencing and an uncertainty fail-closed state.
18. Continuity verifier must be independent from the migrating Principal.
19. Shared-engine deployment requires PrincipalIsolationProfile and FailureDependencyGraph.
20. Epistemic governance requires requester-independent access, stronger independence policy and counterevidence.
21. High-risk Human override must itself pass the high-risk evidence gate.
22. Instruction acceptance must be separated from effect execution.
23. Worker/Grant/WorkOrder limits are invariants, not merely MVP assumptions.
24. Grant expiry requires `IMMEDIATE | DRAIN` semantics.
25. Lifecycle requires orthogonal state axes.
26. Principal Layer code/checkers are themselves World 8 governed artifacts.
27. TCB components must map into existing five World 8 planes; no sixth plane.
28. `World 8 Modification Required: NO` was too absolute: semantic contract remains unchanged, but adapters/bindings/enforcement hooks are required.
29. Proof Registry claims must be narrower than philosophical language.
30. Seven-day scenario is longitudinal operational testing, not a causal scientific experiment.

## Accepted with modification

1. `remediation_owner`: accepted as policy-derived remediation assignment, not a universal first-grant-issuer formula.
2. Risk aggregation: accepted, but no universal scalar `Σ risk × count` formula is frozen.
3. Jurisdiction precedence: accepted as applicability/conflict policy, not universal primary/fallback ranking.
4. Evidence completeness: accepted as compliance against a defined evidence universe, not a claim to all relevant truth.
5. Succession timeout/shutdown: policy-driven, not fixed at 30 days.
6. Incorrect refusal response: review/authority adjustment/remediation, not universal punitive penalty.

## Rejected as universal requirements

1. Research architecture publication must wait for E2. E1 publication is acceptable if executable claims remain OPEN.
2. A continuity witness can never be a Principal. Independence/conflict-of-interest is the actual requirement; the migrating Principal specifically cannot self-verify.
3. A fixed universal succession shutdown duration.
4. Automatic Principal death on breach or insolvency.

## Deferred

Whether the final frozen generation should ultimately remain `World 9` or be classified as a `World 8.x Principal Layer` after executable evidence and prior-art review. The repository uses World 9 as the research/project name without claiming that the generational boundary is scientifically established.
