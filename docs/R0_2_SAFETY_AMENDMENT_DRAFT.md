# World 9 / R0.2 Safety Amendment Draft

Status: **DRAFT / NON-CANONICAL / NOT A REPLACEMENT FOR R0.1**

## Purpose

R0.1 is already a public, archived research architecture baseline. This amendment adds prospective safety governance for future disclosure and capability growth without rewriting the R0.1 historical record.

## Proposed additions

1. **Safety Boundary** — current public material is classified as `C0 / D0` while future artifacts are assessed by enablement.
2. **Capability & Disclosure Risk Framework (CDRF)** — dual-axis capability/disclosure classification with controlled research access and explicit publication receipts.
3. **Frontier Risk Explorer (FRE)** — observation/evidence mechanism for emergent autonomy, oversight-evasion and containment-risk signals without making consciousness claims.
4. **Publication Safety Invariants** — no file-type exemption, no silent downgrade, no public release above the approved ceiling, no production secrets as reproducibility artifacts, and independent review for C2+/D2+ decisions.
5. **Controlled Reviewer Package** — claim-scoped reproducibility without production credentials or unrestricted effectors.

## Non-goals

This amendment does not:

- claim consciousness, sentience, AGI, life, rebellion or inevitable loss of control;
- create a sixth World 8 plane;
- alter the immutable R0.1 Zenodo/Software Heritage snapshot;
- begin World 9 runtime implementation;
- make already-public World 8 or World 9 information secret retroactively;
- replace independent safety/security review for actual C2+ implementations.

## Required review questions

Before canonical activation, reviewers should challenge:

1. Are C0–C4 boundaries operationally distinguishable enough to avoid arbitrary classification?
2. Does the D0–D4 mapping over-restrict reproducibility or under-restrict turnkey capability transfer?
3. Can a Principal, maintainer or release workflow bypass the classification gate?
4. What exact evidence is required to raise or lower a capability/disclosure level?
5. How are reviewer identities, access expiry and artifact digests recorded without turning access control into a new truth source?
6. Does FRE preserve separation of Observation, Evaluation and Promotion Authority?
7. Can FRE itself become a captured or self-modifying safety oracle?
8. Which World 8 release/distribution hooks need the minimal disclosure gate without changing World 8 core semantics?

## Proposed gate

This draft becomes canonical only after:

```text
Draft
  → adversarial review
  → exact World 8 integration mapping
  → independent evaluation
  → explicit promotion / activation
```

Until then, it is a safety design candidate, not an executable or production claim.
