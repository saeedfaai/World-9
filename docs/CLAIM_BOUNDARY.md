# Claim Boundary

R0.1 makes architecture claims, not implementation claims.

Permitted claim:

> World 9 proposes a governed Principal Layer in which canonical Principal identity, obligations, bounded authority, resources and accountability are separated from replaceable cognition and execution engines while remaining subject to World 8 governance.

Not permitted at R0.1:

- production-ready enforcement;
- proven cross-engine continuity;
- proven epistemic independence;
- consciousness/sentience/AGI;
- legal personhood;
- universal superiority;
- established novelty.

Proof language must remain no stronger than the corresponding Proof Registry entry.
