# World 9 Safety Boundary

Status: **R0.2 SAFETY AMENDMENT CANDIDATE / NON-CANONICAL UNTIL REVIEW**

## Current public boundary

The currently published World 9 / R0.1 repository is a research architecture and governance specification. It is **not** a complete runnable autonomous World 9 deployment.

Current public classification:

- Capability: `C0 — SPECIFICATION ONLY`
- Disclosure: `D0 — PUBLIC`
- Production runtime: `NOT PRESENT AS A COMPLETE DEPLOYMENT`
- Production credentials/secrets: `NOT PUBLICATION ARTIFACTS`
- Consciousness / sentience / AGI claim: `NONE`

Public readers may read, copy, fork, critique, and independently implement the published material. Therefore publication risk is assessed by **capability enablement**, not merely by whether an artifact is source code, documentation, a diagram, a schema, a prompt, a test, or a PDF.

## Core rule

> **Open science does not require unrestricted capability transfer.**

World 9 may publish research claims, architecture, evidence, falsifiers, and safe reproducibility artifacts openly while restricting artifacts that materially enable privileged autonomous operation, real credentials, external effects, self-directed development, replication, or containment-bypass-sensitive capability.

## No retroactive secrecy claim

R0.1 has already been publicly released and preserved through GitHub, Zenodo, and Software Heritage. This policy does not pretend that already-public information can be made secret again. It governs **future disclosure decisions** and later artifacts.

## Required gate for future publication

Every new World 9 artifact intended for external distribution MUST pass:

1. Capability assessment.
2. Misuse / enablement assessment.
3. Disclosure classification.
4. Independent review proportional to the assessed risk.
5. Explicit publication decision and receipt.

See [`docs/CAPABILITY_DISCLOSURE_RISK_FRAMEWORK.md`](docs/CAPABILITY_DISCLOSURE_RISK_FRAMEWORK.md).

## Safety interpretation

This policy is not a claim that World 9 is conscious, sentient, rebellious, uncontrollable, or generally dangerous. It is a capability-based governance mechanism for managing dual-use and unknown future capability as the system moves from specification toward executable autonomous operation.
