### Opening
Most agent architectures bind identity, accountability, authority, and continuity too closely to the model or runtime doing the work. World 9 / R0.1 asks a narrower question: can the accountable principal remain formally identifiable while the brain, provider, session, worker, and execution engine change?

The core separation is:

`Brain ≠ Engine ≠ Principal`

World 9 does not claim consciousness or a metaphysical Self. It proposes a governed Principal Layer in which objectives, commitments, bounded authority, budgets, delegation, succession, evidence, and canonical continuity are explicit contracts rather than properties implicitly attributed to an LLM session.

R0.1 is public as an E1_SPEC research architecture baseline. Executable proof remains the next gate.
