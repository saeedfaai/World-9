## Central separation

```text
Brain ≠ Engine ≠ Principal
```

World 9 proposes a governed **Principal Layer** above the World 8 runtime. A Principal is not a model, prompt, session, process, role, or engine. It is a World-8-backed accountable entity whose canonical objectives, commitments, bounded authority, resources, delegation, succession, and formal continuity remain identifiable while cognition and execution substrates are replaced.

World 9 does **not** add a sixth World 8 plane. World 8 keeps exactly five normative planes:

1. Canonical Spine
2. Operational
3. Observation
4. Development / Mason
5. Evidence / Governance

The Principal Layer is a governed client of those planes, never an exemption from them. Its own code, schemas, policies, checkers, and migrations are themselves governed artifacts.

## What R0.1 freezes

- Every Principal is backed by a World 8 Entity; not every Entity is a Principal.
- Principalhood is created by a governed Principal Contract, not inferred from behavior.
- Accountability is layered and attributable, not a vague terminal sink.
- Authority is multidimensional, attenuating, time-bounded, and aggregate-risk-aware.
- WorkOrders narrow work; they do not mint authority.
- `Commitment ≠ Task`.
- Budget accounting includes reservations, binding exposure, and future scheduled exposure.
- Objective refinement separates deterministic structural checks from semantic/evidence-dependent assessment.
- Semantic/LLM assessment cannot turn structural failure into PASS.
- Constraint governance is graph-based, not a universal total ordering.
- HumanRoot is an Office with explicit holder and succession semantics.
- Canonical continuity means exclusive-authority transfer, not metaphysical sameness.
- Shared Engine does not imply shared authority, credentials, budgets, commitments, or failure domains.
- High-risk Human decisions require requester-independent evidence and a counterevidence path.
- Principal Layer code and checkers must themselves pass World 8 governance.

## Claim boundary

R0.1 is a **research architecture baseline**. It does not claim production readiness, consciousness, sentience, AGI, legal personhood, autonomous sovereignty, universal legal interpretation, metaphysical self-sameness, or established novelty.

No executable E2/E3 claim is marked PASS in this repository until actual tests and evidence exist.
